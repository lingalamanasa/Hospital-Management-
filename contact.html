Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions by Hospital Administrators

Everything you need to know about transitioning to Stackly Cloud Health ERP.

How long does a 200-bed hospital take to migrate to Stackly?

Most tertiary hospitals complete complete data migration, staff training, and analyzer interfacing within 7 to 14 days, with zero downtime to active inpatient care.

Does Stackly support our existing lab biochemistry analyzers?

Yes. Stackly LIMS includes pre-built drivers for over 200+ analyzer machines from Roche, Abbott, Beckman Coulter, Sysmex, Siemens, and Mindray via RS-232 and TCP/IP ASTM/HL7 protocols.

Is Stackly compliant with NABH 5th edition and ABDM?

Yes. Stackly comes with pre-configured NABH clinical quality indicators, infection control audit logs, and full Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) Milestone 1, 2, and 3 certifications.

Can our doctors use Stackly on iPads or tablets during rounds?

Absolutely. Stackly is 100% responsive and optimized for iPad, Android tablets, and smartphones with touch-friendly e-prescribing and voice-to-text dictation.

What happens if our hospital local internet connection goes down?

Stackly supports an intelligent Local Offline Sync Cache that allows front desk staff and nurses to record vitals and check in patients even during broadband outages. Data auto-synchronizes the moment the connection restores.

How is patient medical data protected and backed up?

We implement 256-bit AES encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, automated hourly geo-redundant backups, and granular role-based nurse/doctor access controls strictly aligned with HIPAA and ISO 27001.
